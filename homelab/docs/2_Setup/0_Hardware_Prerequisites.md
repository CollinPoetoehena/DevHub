# Setup & Installation: Hardware & Prerequisites

The first step in setting up your home lab is ensuring that you have the necessary hardware and prerequisites in place. This includes selecting the right type of hardware, verifying its specifications, and preparing it for installation.

This document is mainly a link document to [Design: Hardware & Prerequisites](../1_Design/1_Hardware_Prerequisites.md), where you can find detailed information on hardware selection, purchasing strategy, and setup considerations. The information is all explained there because choosing your hardware is a design decision that impacts the rest of your home lab setup. 

So, see [Design: Hardware & Prerequisites](../1_Design/1_Hardware_Prerequisites.md) for all the details regarding hardware selection, purchasing strategy, and setup considerations.
